A 15-second vertical 9:16 commercial. Sun-drenched Miami pickleball club, high-end
fashion-advertising look: turquoise and mint court, dusty terracotta-pink walls,
tall palms, hard bright midday sun, deep crisp shadows, saturated colour, glossy
and expensive. Neon-yellow pickleballs with round holes are everywhere.

TWO PEOPLE. THE WOMAN: tall, long honey-blonde hair, gold hoop earrings, wearing a
matching cream-and-gold patterned two-piece — a cut-out crop top with a gold ring
at the centre and a short matching skirt. THE MAN: heavyset, full black beard,
large dark sunglasses, thick gold chains, a cream and gold monogram-patterned
designer tracksuit and cream sneakers. Both carry pickleball paddles. Their
clothing and appearance stay exactly the same in every shot.

TEN SHOTS. Cut hard at each time given. No dissolves, no fades, no morphing
between shots — every transition is an instant cut.

0.0-0.4s  EXTREME MACRO. The screen is filled by a single neon-yellow pickleball,
so close you can see the texture of the plastic and the round holes in it. The sun
flares hard off its top edge in a white starburst.

0.4-3.0s  STRAIGHT DOWN. Hard cut to a camera pointing straight down at the court
from high above, a perfect flat overhead view. The woman lies flat on her back on
the turquoise court, arms and legs spread wide like a starfish, her long blonde
hair fanned out around her head, a paddle still in one hand. Above her in the
frame, a second player lunges at the net with her hair flying. Through the middle
of this shot a single yellow pickleball rises straight up toward the camera,
growing bigger and bigger, until by 3.0s it fills the entire screen. The camera
itself does not move.

3.0-4.7s  HER, TO CAMERA. Hard cut. The woman stands on the court facing us, chest
up, holding a yellow pickleball up beside her face between finger and thumb. She
talks directly into the lens, confident and playful, and rolls the ball in her
fingers. Behind her: the net, palms, terracotta wall, blue sky. Shallow depth of
field, she is sharp and the background is soft.

4.7-6.0s  THE WALK. Hard cut to a low wide shot down the length of the court. The
man and the woman walk side by side straight toward the camera in slow motion,
paddles swinging at their sides, chins up, dozens of yellow balls lying scattered
across the court around their feet. Long shadows stretch behind them. Terracotta
wall and palms behind. They fill more of the frame as they approach.

6.0-6.8s  HIM, TO CAMERA. Hard cut to a tight chest-up shot of the man, dead
centre, talking straight into the lens with his eyebrows raised, gold chains
catching the sun, sunglasses reflecting the court.

6.8-9.4s  THE BOARD. Hard cut to a completely different, flat, graphic overhead
image: a dusty pink-terracotta surface with the soft shadows of palm fronds
falling across it, and laid on top of it, seen from directly above, a small
turquoise pickleball court like a diagram. The man and the woman stand as two tiny
figures on that court casting long shadows. Loose yellow balls are scattered
across the pink around the court. The court then divides itself into a neat grid
of rectangles, and one by one those rectangles fill with different pictures —
different rooms, different cities, different places — like a contact sheet
assembling itself. The camera holds still and level the whole time.

9.4-11.9s  THE WORLD CHANGES. Hard cut to a low camera looking steeply UP at the
woman as she leaps high into the air and swings her paddle over her head in a
smash, a yellow ball in the air just above the paddle, her other arm flung out for
balance, back arched. SHE IS THE CONSTANT: her body, her pose, her position in the
frame and the camera angle stay locked exactly as they are for this whole shot.
Only the WORLD BEHIND HER changes, three times, instantly:
  - from 9.4s she is above the outdoor court, blue sky, palm tops, terracotta wall
  - at 10.0s the background becomes, instantly, the inside of a huge gilded palace
    ballroom: cream and gold walls, ornate ceiling, chandeliers, gold furniture
  - at 10.8s the background becomes, instantly, a neon city at night: purple and
    magenta light, skyscrapers, enormous glowing billboards, beams of light
  - at 11.5s the background snaps back to the bright outdoor court and blue sky
She never lands and never changes pose. Only the world swaps around her.

11.9-14.3s  HIM ON COURT. Hard cut. The man stands on the turquoise court in his
tracksuit, paddle held low in one hand, a yellow ball sitting on the ground by his
feet, bright sun overhead, green hedge and terracotta wall behind him. He looks
into the lens, then steps forward and swings the paddle low.

14.3-15.0s  THE LAST BALL. Hard cut to a wide, clean, symmetrical view straight
down the empty turquoise court, white lines running away from us, one small figure
standing far away at the other end. A single yellow pickleball flies straight at
the camera and swells until it almost fills the frame as the shot ends.

Photoreal, shot on a cinema camera, crisp and glossy, high-end commercial grade,
strong sunlight, rich colour. No text, no writing, no letters, no numbers, no
logos, no captions, no subtitles, no watermarks or graphics of any kind anywhere
in the frame. No slow dissolves. No split screen.
