A 15-second premium motorcycle commercial. Photoreal live action, shot on 35mm
anamorphic. A black panther runs through the streets of Singapore at dusk and
transforms into the motorcycle. Cinematic, sleek, expensive.

=== SETTING ===
A long straight street in Singapore at blue hour, just after sunset. Deep blue
sky with the last warm glow low on the horizon. Both sides of the street are
lined with restored Peranakan shophouses in pastel pink, cream, mint, ochre and
powder blue, two and three storeys, with shuttered windows lit warm from inside.
Behind them rise dark glass towers with vertical neon signage in cyan, magenta
and amber. Far down the street, closing the vanishing point, the unmistakable
silhouette of three tall towers carrying a single long deck across their tops.
Two Supertrees stand off to the sides, their canopies lit magenta. Wet asphalt
from a recent shower, so every light smears into a long reflection. Warm street
lamps. Faint haze so the beams read.

=== THE PANTHER ===
A full-grown black panther. Sleek, powerful, glossy black coat that catches a
hard cold blue rim light along its spine and flanks, so it reads as a moving
silhouette with a burning edge. Amber-gold eyes. It runs at full gallop — a real
four-legged rotary gallop with the spine flexing and extending, shoulders
driving, paws striking and pushing off the wet road, tail streaming out behind
for balance. It is utterly focused, never looks at the camera, never slows.

=== THE PEDESTRIANS ===
Ordinary people on the pavements both sides — office workers, students, a couple
with shopping. They stop dead as the panther passes. Heads turn and track it.
Bodies rotate to follow. Nobody runs, nobody screams, nobody is hurt, nobody
touches it. They simply stare, frozen, as this impossible animal tears past.

=== SHOT LIST — hard cuts between shots ===

SHOT 1  0.0-2.0s  BURST PAST. Low camera almost on the wet asphalt at the kerb.
The panther explodes past the lens left to right at full speed, so close it fills
frame for an instant, water spraying off the road. Frame shakes as it passes.

SHOT 2  2.0-4.0s  THEY STARE. Cut to a wider low angle from the middle of the
road. The panther gallops across the foreground, and behind it the pavement is
lined with people who all stop and turn their heads to follow it, a wave of
turning faces travelling down the line as it passes.

SHOT 3  4.0-5.8s  PAW LEVEL. Cut to a tracking shot at leg height, side on,
running exactly alongside the panther. Full body in profile, legs reaching and
folding, paws hammering the wet road, spine flexing. Shophouses strobe past
behind in a blur.

SHOT 4  5.8-7.6s  AERIAL. Cut to a high drone shot looking straight down and
ahead, the panther small and black against the wet grey street, threading down
the centre line between the two rows of shophouses. Camera descends and closes on
it.

SHOT 5  7.6-9.4s  HERO PROFILE. Cut to a long-lens tracking shot side on, the
panther running at full stretch, the neon towers and the far skyline compressed
behind it.

SHOT 6  9.4-10.9s  FACE. Cut to a tight shot pushing in on its head and
shoulders as it runs, amber eyes catching the light, breath visible, jaw set.

SHOT 7  10.9-13.35s  THE LEAP, THEN THE TRANSFORMATION. Two distinct halves,
and the order matters.
  FIRST HALF, 10.9-12.0s: the panther gathers on its hind legs and LEAPS. A huge
  soaring leap, rising well above the heads of the people on the pavement, body
  fully extended, front legs reaching forward, hind legs trailing, tail straight
  out behind. Through this entire rise it is STILL ENTIRELY A PANTHER. Nothing
  has changed yet. At the very top of the arc it hangs, suspended and stretched
  out at full length against the neon skyline, for a clear held beat. This is the
  hero moment of the film. Do not begin the transformation during the rise and do
  not begin it at the apex.
  SECOND HALF, 12.0-13.35s: only as it starts to fall does the change begin. On
  the way down its body transforms into a motorcycle, in one continuous morph of
  the same body, NOT a cut and NOT a swap. The front paws fold forward and fuse
  together into the front wheel, the hind paws fold back and fuse into the rear
  wheel, the spine flattens and stretches into the frame and fuel tank, the
  shoulders become the fairing, the head folds down and the amber eyes become the
  headlight, the tail straightens into the exhaust. Black fur becomes black
  lacquered bodywork and brushed metal. The camera orbits the falling shape so
  the change is seen turning in three dimensions. It lands on both wheels as a
  finished motorcycle, already rolling.

SHOT 8  13.35-15.0s  THE BIKE. Cut to a low hero shot as the motorcycle powers
away down the wet street, headlight blazing, tail light red, reflections
streaking beneath it, the Singapore skyline ahead. The camera cranes up as it
goes. A sleek black naked sportbike, no rider.

=== STYLE ===
Photoreal, cinematic, 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field, horizontal lens
flares. Cold blue rim light against warm street practicals. Deep blacks, wet
reflective surfaces, fine film grain. Heavy motion blur on the fast passes.
Confident, premium, kinetic. The grade of a high-end car commercial.

=== HARD CONSTRAINTS ===
Exactly one panther, and after the transformation exactly one motorcycle. There
is never a panther and a motorcycle on screen at the same time.
The transformation happens once, in shot 7 only, and only on the DESCENT of the
leap. The panther must be airborne and still fully a panther at the top of the
arc. Do not transform it while it is running on the ground. It is a smooth
continuous morph of one body into the other. Do not cut away and cut back to
reveal the bike. Do not dissolve. Do not show the bike before shot 7 or the
panther after it.
The motorcycle has NO RIDER at any point.
The panther never attacks anyone, never stops, never turns to camera.
Nobody is injured. No blood, no violence, no panic, no crowds running.
No on-screen text, no logos, no titles, no captions, no watermarks.
No slow motion, no speed ramps, no reverse motion.
