REFERENCE ROLES. Image1 is the house above ground. Image2 is the same house
below ground in the Upside Down. Image3 is the plot above ground. Image4 is the
same plot below ground. Take ALL appearance from Image1-Image4.

Image5-Image9 are camera blocking diagrams from a rough 3D previz. Use them ONLY
to read camera height, angle, framing and how far the picture is rotated at each
beat. Completely ignore their colours, materials, simple shapes and toy-like
look. Never render anything that looks like Image5-Image9.

SCENE. Hawkins, Indiana, 1983. A large two-storey suburban family home, and the
identical house mirrored directly beneath it underground in the Upside Down. One
unbroken take travelling from one world into the other. Above: hard afternoon
sun, deep blue sky, green lawn. Below: ashen grey, wet tendrils and vine, dull
red window seep, spores drifting, churning black and blood-red storm sky, silent
lightning, nothing alive.

CAMERA. One continuous Steadicam take, no cuts, always aimed at the house.

THE RULE: once the camera begins to climb, its travel and its rotation happen
together as one inseparable motion. The picture turns steadily the entire time
the camera is moving. The rotation never pauses and never happens as a separate
move after arriving. One smooth half-turn spread evenly across the journey.

BEAT 1 - OPEN LOW AND PULL BACK. Start exactly as framed in Image5: eye level,
two metres off the ground, close enough that the front door and porch fill the
frame. This is a ground-level shot, NOT an aerial one. Pull straight back at
that same low height until the framing matches Image6: whole house, lawn and
driveway in frame, ground line across the middle, horizon dead level. No
rotation at all through this beat. This is the longest beat of the shot.

BEAT 2 - RISE AND TURN AS ONE. Without stopping, begin climbing and turning in
the same instant, arcing up over the house until the framing matches Image7:
high above the roof looking down, picture rotated a quarter turn, horizon
running vertically.

BEAT 3 - THROUGH THE GROUND. Still turning at the same steady rate, carry over
the ridge, drop fast behind the house and pass straight down through the ground
plane, reaching the framing of Image8 at the moment of breaking through. Note it
is already well past a quarter turn there and still turning. It must not be
level.

BEAT 4 - BENEATH. Below ground now, keep descending until far under the mirrored
house looking up at its underside, then swing back up toward its front. The
rotation crosses past halfway during this beat.

BEAT 5 - ARRIVE ALREADY RIGHT WAY UP. Rise to face the front of the underground
house and complete the half turn at the exact moment of arrival, matching
Image9: camera fully inverted, so the underground house reads upright and level,
the mirror of the opening. Settle and hold.

DO NOT start the shot in the air or as a drone flyover. DO NOT hold level
through the descent then rotate at the end. DO NOT arrive with the house upside
down and spin it upright.

STYLE. Photoreal, anamorphic, 35mm grain, Stranger Things cinematography. Single
take, smooth constant motion, no cuts, no shake. No people, animals, vehicles or
text.
