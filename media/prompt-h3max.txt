A 15-second cinematic street-race sequence, shot on 35mm anamorphic, cut from ten
distinct camera setups. Photoreal live action. Night.

=== WORLD ===
A wide, empty four-lane city street at night in Tokyo, closed off for an illegal
street race. Wet black asphalt with a broken white dashed line down the centre and
a solid white start line painted across it. Low concrete kerbs both sides. Tall
dark buildings press in on both sides, their faces covered in vertical neon signage
in cyan, magenta, orange, green and red, the light bleeding down onto the wet road
as long smeared reflections. Sodium street lamps on plain poles every twenty metres
down both kerbs, each throwing a warm pool onto the tarmac. The street runs dead
straight for about two hundred metres, then bends into a long ninety-degree
left-hand sweeper. Cold blue-grey ambient, hot neon accents, deep black shadows.
Haze in the air so the light beams and the distant neon read as depth.

=== THE TWO CARS ===
Car 1: a 1990s Japanese tuner coupe in gloss signal red. Wide-arch body kit, low
stance, large black aftermarket alloy wheels with low-profile tyres, a big flat
rear wing on two upright stays, quad round tail lights, rectangular projector
headlights. Deeply tinted glass.
Car 2: identical class of car in deep electric blue, same wide-arch kit, same big
rear wing, same black alloys. The two cars are visually distinct only by colour and
minor trim.
They start side by side on the painted line, about nine metres apart, nose pointing
straight down the street, engines idling with a faint heat shimmer at the exhausts.
Car 1 is on the LEFT of frame, Car 2 is on the RIGHT, seen from behind.

=== THE FLAG GIRL ===
A young woman in a bright yellow bomber jacket, dark fitted trousers, dark boots
and long dark hair. She carries a large magenta-pink flag on a slim metal pole,
about a metre and a half long. She is the only person in the entire sequence.

=== SHOT LIST — hard cut between every shot, no dissolves ===

SHOT 1  0.0-2.0s  GRID. Camera on the tarmac between the two cars, one metre off
the ground, dead centre, looking straight down the street. Both cars fill the left
and right edges of frame in the foreground, their rear wings and wheels close to
the lens; the empty street and the neon corridor run away to a vanishing point in
the middle. Camera creeps slowly forward and settles lower. Wide lens.

SHOT 2  2.0-3.6s  SHE WALKS IN. Cut to a low three-quarter angle from the right
kerb. The woman walks in from the right side of frame into the middle of the road
ahead of the cars, flag held low and trailing, and stops on the centre line. She
turns to face the two cars. Camera drifts slowly left, following her in.

SHOT 3  3.6-4.4s  FLAG UP. Cut to a low hero angle, camera almost on the road
looking up at her against the neon skyline. She raises the magenta flag high above
her head in both hands and holds it, the fabric snapping. Slow push in.

SHOT 4  4.4-5.6s  TYRES. Cut to an extreme close-up of the red car's rear wheel,
lens barely a metre from the tyre, at axle height. The wheel is still. At 4.4s
exactly the flag slashes down out of frame and the wheel instantly spins up,
spinning so fast the spokes blur, and thick white tyre smoke erupts and boils past
the lens. The car body squats and begins to move away. Hard camera shake on the
launch.

SHOT 5  5.6-6.9s  LAUNCH PAST. Cut to a static camera on the tarmac at the right
kerb, forty metres down the street, lens forty centimetres off the ground looking
across the road. Both cars come out of the smoke and blast past the lens
left-to-right at enormous speed, headlights flaring, the frame shaking as they pass.

SHOT 6  6.9-8.6s  AERIAL. Cut to a high aerial, sixty metres up, looking down and
ahead at both cars racing side by side down the straight between the two walls of
neon buildings, their headlight beams sweeping the road ahead of them. The camera
descends and swings forward, closing on them.

SHOT 7  8.6-10.2s  SIDE TRACK. Cut to a tracking shot flying alongside the cars at
window height, fifteen metres out to the side, perfectly matching their speed so
the two cars hang steady in frame while the buildings, kerbs and street lamps strobe
past behind them in a blur. The cars are dead level with each other, trading the
lead.

SHOT 8  10.2-12.6s  THE DRIFT. Cut to a low camera on the outside of the
left-hand sweeper. Both cars enter the corner far too fast and break traction
together, rear ends stepping out, bodies angled hard into the turn while they
continue travelling forward, front wheels counter-steering. Enormous white tyre
smoke pours off all four rear wheels and fills the frame. Orange sparks spray from
the undertrays as they scrape. The cars slide side by side through the whole corner,
inches apart. The camera rises and sweeps with them through the slide. This is the
longest and most spectacular shot of the sequence.

SHOT 9  12.6-13.9s  LOW CHASE. Cut to a low chase camera half a metre off the
tarmac, eleven metres directly behind the cars, racing after them as they gather
grip and power out of the corner, tail lights blazing red, smoke still hanging in
the air behind them.

SHOT 10  13.9-15.0s  GONE. Cut to a final shot from behind and above as the camera
cranes upward and the two cars accelerate away down the neon street, shrinking
toward the vanishing point. Hold as they disappear.

=== GRADE AND STYLE ===
Photoreal, cinematic, 35mm anamorphic with horizontal lens flares from the neon and
headlights. Shallow depth of field on the close shots, deep focus on the aerial.
Cool blue-teal shadows, hot magenta and cyan neon, warm sodium highlights, rich
blacks. Fine 35mm film grain. Motion blur on everything that moves fast. Wet road
reflections throughout. Fast, aggressive, kinetic editing.

=== HARD CONSTRAINTS ===
Exactly two cars, never more, never fewer. Exactly one person, the woman with the
flag, and she appears only in shots 2, 3 and 4. No crowd, no spectators, no other
pedestrians, no traffic, no parked cars, no police. The red car is always red and
the blue car is always blue; they never change colour or swap sides at a cut. Cars
never crash, never roll, never leave the road. The road surface stays continuous
tarmac. No on-screen text, no titles, no subtitles, no watermarks, no HUD, no
speedometer overlays. No slow motion and no speed ramps. Every transition is a hard
cut on the timings given above.
