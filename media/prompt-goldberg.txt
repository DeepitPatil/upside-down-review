A 15-second Rube Goldberg chain reaction filmed as ONE SINGLE UNBROKEN
CONTINUOUS TAKE. Photoreal live action, macro tabletop cinematography.

=== THE ONE RULE ===
There is exactly one shot. The camera never cuts, never jumps, never dissolves,
and never resets. It runs continuously from the first frame to the last, gliding
smoothly left to right along the machine on a motion-control dolly, always
keeping the currently-firing part of the machine centred in frame. If you are
tempted to cut, do not — move the camera instead.

=== SETTING ===
A long dark workbench in a dim workshop, photographed at tabletop height. Matte
charcoal bench surface, a plain dark grey back wall a few metres behind it, deep
shadow beyond. Warm practical light from above and slightly behind camera, cool
blue fill from the left, so every object is crisply modelled with a soft warm
key and a cold rim. Shallow depth of field: the firing part is sharp, the rest
of the bench falls gently out of focus. Fine dust drifting in the light beams.

=== THE MACHINE, LEFT TO RIGHT ===
Everything sits in one straight line along the bench so a single dolly move can
follow the whole chain.
1. A pale grey wooden chute with metal side rails, descending in a shallow
   S-curve from high on the left down to bench level.
2. A bright orange wooden seesaw plank balanced on a small metal fulcrum, with a
   raised lip at each end.
3. A line of fourteen white rectangular dominoes standing upright, evenly spaced,
   running left to right along the bench.
4. A slim metal release lever, and behind it a tall dark post carrying a blue
   polished steel pendulum ball on a thin cable.
5. A green metal bucket and a purple metal counterweight hanging from opposite
   ends of one rope over a pulley wheel at the top of a mast.
6. A pale grey chute hinged at its left end, currently level, with a yellow
   ball resting in it.
7. A cyan bladed turbine wheel on a stand, free to spin.
8. A closed dark box with a folded magenta flag on a pole inside it.

=== THE CHAIN, IN STRICT ORDER ===
Each event is caused by the one before it. Nothing moves before the thing that
triggers it has arrived. Nothing moves late.

0.0s   A polished red marble is released at the top of the chute and rolls down,
       accelerating, following the S-curve, rattling between the rails.
2.3s   The marble drops onto the raised left end of the orange seesaw. The seesaw
       tips under its weight and the marble rolls down the plank.
3.4s   The marble runs off the low end of the seesaw onto the bench.
4.0s   The marble strikes the first white domino and knocks it over.
4.0-6.0s The dominoes fall one after another in a clean travelling wave down the
       line, each knocking the next, the wave moving left to right.
6.2s   The final domino falls onto the metal release lever and pushes it down.
6.4s   The lever releases the blue pendulum ball, which swings down and across in
       a long arc.
7.8s   The pendulum ball strikes the green bucket.
7.9-9.6s The struck bucket falls, and because it is roped over the pulley, the
       purple counterweight rises on the other side at the same rate.
9.6s   The risen counterweight knocks the hinged chute, which tips downward.
10.4s  The yellow ball rolls out of the tipped chute and runs down it, gathering
       speed.
11.9s  The yellow ball slams into the cyan turbine, which spins up fast, blades
       blurring.
13.4s  The spinning turbine drives the box open and a magenta flag rises
       smoothly out of it on its pole and unfurls.
15.0s  The flag stands fully raised. End of shot.

=== CAMERA ===
One continuous motion-control move, left to right, travelling roughly forty
metres of bench over fifteen seconds. It starts close and low on the red marble
at the top of the chute, then tracks right, staying with whatever is currently
firing. It rises slightly and pulls back a little to take in the full line of
dominoes as the wave travels, comes back down and closer for the pendulum swing,
cranes up as the bucket falls and the counterweight rises, then descends and
pushes in for the turbine, and finishes with a slow push in on the rising flag.
The move is smooth and unhurried throughout, never whip-panning, never stopping
dead. Slight parallax as it travels, so the bench and back wall shift against
each other.

=== STYLE ===
Photoreal, macro tabletop cinematography, shot on 50mm with shallow depth of
field. Crisp materials: polished glass marble, painted wood, brushed metal,
matte plastic. Realistic contact shadows on the bench. Physically believable
weight and momentum in every collision. Warm key, cool fill, rich blacks, fine
film grain. No stylisation, no cartoon look.

=== HARD CONSTRAINTS ===
ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT. No cuts, no dissolves, no jump cuts, no camera resets, no
speed ramps, no slow motion, no reverse motion.
The red marble is the same single red marble throughout stages 1 to 4 — it never
changes colour, size or shape, and there is never more than one red marble.
Events happen strictly in the order and at the times given. No domino falls
before the marble reaches it. The pendulum does not swing before the lever is
tripped. The bucket does not fall before the pendulum strikes it. The flag does
not appear before the turbine spins.
Exactly fourteen dominoes. No hands, no people, no animals anywhere in frame.
No on-screen text, no titles, no captions, no watermarks, no arrows or diagrams.
Nothing explodes, nothing catches fire, nothing shatters.
